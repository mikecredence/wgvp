OWN03: one take of one of us walking back and forth between two chalk lines 13.5 ft (4.115 m) apart, centre to centre, filmed by two still cameras at once: a tablet on a box looking across the walk (left panel, landscape) and a phone on the ground looking along it (right panel, portrait). The two files were lined up on a hand clap and tiled side by side. The published file is half the recorded size (1516 x 960, 30 fps, 31 s); house numbers are covered with flat patches and a passer-by on the far road is painted out with the empty background (the cameras do not move), so nobody but the walker is in it. The recording CSV is fabricated from the walker's pelvis motion in the side view, as for the other examples, with IMU t = 0 planted 0.8 s into the clip.
